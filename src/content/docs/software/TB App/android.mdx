Our android app is currently only sideloadable. 

## How to Install

- download the APK from our website
- Once downloaded, Tap "Install", "open" or "Run" depending on your device and browser (Some devices/browsers may not give you the option of running it directly, if this is the case then tap on the downloaded file from your downloads folder)
- On the window that opens, Tap "Install"
- Your phone may warn you about sideloading apps; this is a standard feature of android and says nothing about the app itself.
- Tap "More Information" and then "Install Anyway"
- Tap "Done" to close, or "Open" to open the app.

However, In 2027, Google is adding another barrier to sideloading. When that happens we will publish another guide on how to do it, that process will only need to be done once and you can sideload any app like normal

## Main Navigation

Our navbar shows the FIRST FOUR navigation options; the rest are shown in the "More" tab. This tab is not a seperate navigation option, its simply a door to the rest.

| Tab | Description |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Home page, shows MOTD and app updates/known issues |
| **News** | Shows the 5 most recent blog posts and a button to visit our blog archive. |
| **Products | See our full range of products, organised by category - Links open in your default browser |
| **Chat with Miles** | Talk to our support chatbot to get quick answers to common questions |
| **Support** | Contact our support team & get help with various issues |
| **Status** | Check the status of our services |
| **Settings** | Find various app settings - see [Settings](#settings) for more info |
| **Legal** | Read our Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy and other legal documents |
